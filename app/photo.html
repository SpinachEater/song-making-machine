Tips for good photos
  • Hold the phone straight above the page, so the staff lines look parallel.
  • Use daylight or a bright lamp, and avoid your own shadow across the page.
  • Take one photo per page and add them in order. You can move pages up or down.
  • Printed music reads best, and a PDF of a printed score reads best of all. Handwritten music is not supported yet.
  • The reader picks up notes and rhythms on treble and bass clef. Dynamics, articulations and lyrics are not read yet.
