Hum to Piano

Hum or sing a tune. The app finds each note, shows it on a piano roll and as sheet music, and plays it back on piano.

Key and tempo are worked out from your humming. Tap a note on the staff to hear it.
    Tips for clean results
    • Use a quiet room and hold the phone about 30 cm from your mouth.
    • Hum each note steadily. A tiny break or a "d" between repeated notes helps the app count them.
    • The number under each note is how far you were from the exact pitch, in cents (100 cents is one semitone). Tap a note to hear it.
    • "Allow for overall sharp or flat" helps when you sing a little away from A = 440 Hz, as unaccompanied singing often does. On long songs it also follows you if you slowly drift flat or sharp.
